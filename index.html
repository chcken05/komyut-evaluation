How this works

  • You do not log in to anything or install anything. Our team runs a live screen-share demo with a passenger phone, a driver phone and the admin panel. A recording of the same walkthrough is also available.
  • During the demo the tricycle shed queue is switched on: passengers book from a shed and the system offers each request to the driver first in line.
  • For each item, mark what you saw:
    • Pass: you saw it work as described.
    • Fail: it was shown but did not work as described. Please add a short note.
    • Not shown: it was not demonstrated, or you could not see it clearly.
  • Notes are optional. Your answers save automatically as you go. It takes about 15 to 20 minutes alongside the demo.
  • When you finish, download your copy and send it as described at the end of the page.
